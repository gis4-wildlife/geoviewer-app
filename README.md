# viewer-app

Sky is a whale shark (*Rhincodon typus*) satellite-tagged by the **Galápagos Whale Shark Project®** in the Galápagos Marine Reserve. Between August 2021 and February 2022, Sky was tracked for nearly six months on a remarkable open-ocean journey — departing the waters around the Galápagos archipelago and venturing thousands of kilometers across the eastern Pacific, reaching latitudes as far south as 17°S before the signal was last recorded. This biologging dataset, collected during field expeditions in one of the world's most biodiverse marine protected areas, offers a rare glimpse into the long-distance movement ecology of the largest fish on Earth.

The dataset is made available by **GIS4 Wildlife** as part of the Wildlife Tracker GEO demo platform. To access the data, submit a request through the Geoviewer:

https://geoviewer.gis4-wildlife.com
